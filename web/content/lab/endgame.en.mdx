---
title: Endgame
description: Hard while the deck lasts, then perfect play. The Hard level.
---

When the deck runs out each player has three cards left, and each knows exactly which cards the other holds: the
ones not yet played. From there the game has perfect information, and the last three tricks can be worked out to
the end.

Endgame does this:

- **While the deck lasts** it plays like [Hard](/en/lab/hard).
- **Once the deck is empty** it tries each of its cards and, for each, every possible continuation, assuming the
  opponent plays its best in turn. It plays the card it ends with the most points with; ties go to the lowest index.

The result is exact: no strategy of the opponent takes those points away from it.

On the site, once the deck is empty Endgame's reasoning shows each of its cards with the points it would end with,
and the forecast on the bar. Before that it shows Hard's rule.

## In the arena

Over 10 000 duplicate deals, playing the last three tricks well is enough to beat Hard: 56.3% ± 0.3. Against the
bots that also simulate the rest of the game it loses:

| Against | Endgame's score | Margin per game |
| ------- | --------------- | --------------- |
| PIMC    | 35.4% ± 0.6     | −9.6 points     |
| ISMCTS  | 37.6% ± 0.7     | −8.3 points     |

The interval against Hard is narrow, ± 0.3, because the two bots play the same cards while the deck lasts and part
only in the last three tricks.
