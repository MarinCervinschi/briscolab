---
title: Random
description: Plays a random card among the legal ones. It is the floor.
---

Random picks with equal probability among the cards it may play. It looks at neither the table, the briscola nor
the points.

It is there for one thing: to give the arena a floor. A bot that does not beat Random has a bug, not a weak
strategy.

## In the arena

It loses to everyone, by a lot. Over 10 000 duplicate deals:

| Against | Random's score | Margin per game |
| ------- | -------------- | --------------- |
| Greedy  | 10.9% ± 0.4    | −43.5 points    |
| Hard    | 13.0% ± 0.5    | −37.4 points    |
| Endgame | 10.3% ± 0.4    | −40.8 points    |
| PIMC    | 9.3% ± 0.4     | −45.2 points    |
| ISMCTS  | 8.2% ± 0.4     | −45.9 points    |

Random does a little better against Hard than against Greedy. Hard does not spend briscole on tricks without a
carico, so it leaves Random some tricks that Greedy would take.

It has no level on the site: even for a beginner, Greedy is a more useful opponent.
