---
title: ISMCTS
description: A search tree built over 1000 imagined deals. The Expert level.
---

ISMCTS stands for _information-set Monte Carlo tree search_, in the single-observer version of Cowling, Powley and
Whitehouse (2012). Like [PIMC](/en/lab/pimc) it imagines deals consistent with what it knows, but instead of trying
each card on all of them it builds one tree of the cards both sides play, from its own point of view.

## One iteration

1. **It imagines a new deal**, consistent with what it has seen.
2. **It goes down the tree** among the cards legal in that deal, choosing by UCB1: it prefers the cards that have
   paid best so far, but also tries those tried little.
3. **It adds one card** to the tree.
4. **It finishes the game** as PIMC does: Hard for both sides while the deck lasts, then exact play.
5. **It reports the result** to every card on the path: the share of the 120 points the side that played it ends
   with.

After 1000 iterations it plays its most visited card. Once the deck is empty it plays exactly, like
[Endgame](/en/lab/endgame).

UCB1's exploration constant is 0.1, chosen in the arena. With the usual value, 0.7, the cards differ by a few
hundredths and the search explored too much: with 1000 iterations it lost 45% to 55% against PIMC.

## On the site

It is the Expert level. The bot thinks in the browser, in a Web Worker, for at most one second per card; then it
plays the best card found so far. The reasoning shows the forecast, the iterations, some of the hands it imagines
for you and the cards it weighs.

## In the arena

Over 10 000 duplicate deals it is level with PIMC, 50.1% ± 0.7, and beats every other bot:

| Against | ISMCTS's score | Margin per game |
| ------- | -------------- | --------------- |
| Random  | 91.8% ± 0.4    | +45.9 points    |
| Greedy  | 64.2% ± 0.7    | +9.8 points     |
| Hard    | 68.6% ± 0.6    | +12.8 points    |
| Endgame | 62.4% ± 0.7    | +8.3 points     |
| PIMC    | 50.1% ± 0.7    | +0.1 points     |
