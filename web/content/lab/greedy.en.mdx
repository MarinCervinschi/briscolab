Greedy looks one card ahead: the trick in progress. It takes no account of the cards already played or of those
still to come.

## How it chooses

- **Leading**, it plays its cheapest card.
- **Answering**, it works out for each card what it gets from this trick: the points on the table if the card wins,
  the same points negative if it loses. It plays the best card.
- **When no card wins, or none is worth it**, it gives the card that gives away the fewest points.

Ties go to the cheapest card: non-briscole first, then points, then strength. It is deterministic: in the same
position it always plays the same card.

On the site, Greedy's reasoning is the sentence of the rule it used.

## In the arena

Over 10 000 duplicate deals it beats Random, 89.1% ± 0.4, and loses to every other bot:

| Against | Greedy's score | Margin per game |
| ------- | -------------- | --------------- |
| Hard    | 36.5% ± 0.7    | −9.3 points     |
| Endgame | 34.7% ± 0.7    | −10.1 points    |
| PIMC    | 37.5% ± 0.7    | −8.4 points     |
| ISMCTS  | 35.8% ± 0.7    | −9.8 points     |

Greedy spends a briscola even to take two points, because it looks only at this trick. Hard, which beats it, keeps
its briscole for the carichi.
