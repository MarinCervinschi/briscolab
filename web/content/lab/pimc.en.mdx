---
title: PIMC
description: Imagines 100 possible deals and tries each card on all of them. The arena's champion.
---

PIMC stands for _perfect-information Monte Carlo_. The bot does not know which cards the opponent holds or the
order of the deck, but it knows which cards it has not seen yet. So it imagines:

1. **It deals at random** the cards it does not know between the opponent's hand and the deck, consistent with
   everything it has seen. That gives one possible deal, face up. It prepares 100.
2. **It tries each of its cards** on all 100. After the card, the game goes on with Hard for both sides while the
   deck lasts, then with [Endgame](/en/lab/endgame)'s exact play.
3. **It plays the card** it ends with the most points with, summed over the 100 deals.

Every card is tried on the same 100 deals, so the difference between two cards does not depend on which deals came
up. Once the deck is empty it plays exactly, like Endgame. Its generator starts from a seed, so a game can be
replayed exactly.

The known limit of the method: in the imagined deals the bot sees every card, and can count on information it would
not have at the table.

## In the arena

It is the champion: over 10 000 duplicate deals it beats every bot except ISMCTS, with which it is level.

| Against | PIMC's score | Margin per game |
| ------- | ------------ | --------------- |
| Random  | 90.7% ± 0.4  | +45.2 points    |
| Greedy  | 62.5% ± 0.7  | +8.4 points     |
| Hard    | 69.9% ± 0.6  | +13.4 points    |
| Endgame | 64.6% ± 0.6  | +9.6 points     |
| ISMCTS  | 49.9% ± 0.7  | −0.1 points     |

It has no level on the site: the Expert level plays ISMCTS, which is as strong and can stop after any number of
simulations.
